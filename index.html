Questions people actually ask

Which markets are covered?

Area scoring and the hotspot map are live in Denver, Phoenix, Nashville, Charlotte, Raleigh and Tampa. Property search runs in Austin too, though its scoring isn't live yet. Market intel, LOI, pipeline, CRM and underwriting work anywhere.

Is this a CRM, an underwriting tool, or a lead source?

All three, on purpose. Splitting them is what makes people re-key the same address into four tools. Here the score, the pro-forma, the LOI and the pipeline stage are one record.

How does the scoring work?

Neighborhoods are rated from median income, rent, rent burden and density. Deals get a weighted 0–100 blending underwriting metrics with live market signals. Both refresh as the underlying feeds do.

What happens if I cancel?

You drop to Free at the end of the period. Deals, contacts and LOIs are preserved — you just lose the Pro surfaces. Deletion only happens if you ask for it.

How is my data secured?

Firebase Auth for sign-in, Firestore security rules scoped to your account, Stripe for payments. We never store card data.

The record refreshes tonight either way.

You can read it, or you can be the person calling the owner who already read it.
