Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Talk Studio LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when you use our website, submit a form, or exchange text messages with us. By using our website or providing your information, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Information about your property (county, city, or parcel — optional)
  • Whether you have consented to receive text messages from us

We also automatically record limited technical details with any form submission — the date and time, your IP address, and your browser's user agent — solely to keep an accurate, auditable record of consent and to help prevent abuse.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • Contact you about your property and a potential cash offer;
  • Respond to your questions and requests;
  • Send you text messages if you have opted in to receive them; and
  • Maintain records of consent as required by applicable law and carrier rules.

Text messaging and mobile opt-in

Consent to receive text messages is optional and is never a condition of any purchase, offer, or use of our website. If you opt in, message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can reply HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your mobile information. No mobile information (including your phone number) or text-messaging opt-in and consent data will be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of information sharing described in this policy.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only with service providers who help us operate our website and communications (for example, our messaging and hosting providers), and only to the extent needed to provide those services on our behalf. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights. As stated above, mobile opt-in and consent data is never shared for third-party marketing.

Data retention

We keep the information you provide for as long as needed to contact you about your property, to maintain records of consent, and to comply with our legal obligations. You may ask us to delete your information using the contact details below.

Your choices

  • Opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us.
  • Decline to provide information — though some of it is needed for us to respond.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.
