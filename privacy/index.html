Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Talk Studio LLC collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the land-offer request form at smartinvestorcrm.com/sell-your-land.

Information we collect

We only collect what you type into our form, plus basic technical details sent by your browser:

  • Name and phone number — required to respond to your request.
  • Email address and property location — optional.
  • SMS consent — whether you checked the optional text-message box, the exact wording you agreed to, and the date and time you submitted it.
  • Technical data — your IP address and browser user agent, recorded with the submission so we have an auditable record of when and from where consent was given.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics on this site, and we do not buy or append data about you from other sources.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your property and a potential cash offer.
  • To send you text messages only if you checked the optional SMS consent box.
  • To keep a record of your consent, as required by mobile carriers and applicable law.

We do not use your information for any other purpose, and we do not send automated marketing blasts.

Text messages

Checking the SMS box is optional. It is not required to submit the form, to receive an offer, or as a condition of any purchase. If you leave it unchecked we will reach out by phone or email instead.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

Full messaging terms are on our Terms & Conditions page.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes of any kind.

We share your information only with service providers that operate our site and messaging on our behalf — our web host, our messaging provider, and our database provider — and only to the extent they need it to deliver that service. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

How long we keep it

We retain submissions and consent records for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and to document your consent, and then for a reasonable period afterward to meet record-keeping obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below.

Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any message, or contact us directly.
  • Stop all contact: email or call us and we will remove you from our list.
  • Access or delete your data: contact us and we will honor the request.

Security

Submissions are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in access-controlled systems. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect what you give us.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be posted on this page.
